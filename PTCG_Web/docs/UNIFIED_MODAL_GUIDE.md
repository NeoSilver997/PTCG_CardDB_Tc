# PTCG Card Management - Unified Modal System

## Overview
The PTCG Card Search application now features a **unified card detail modal** that handles both deck building and inventory management in one convenient location.

## How to Use

### 🎯 **Accessing Card Actions**
1. **Click on any card** in the card grid
2. The **Card Detail Modal** will open showing:
   - Full card details and stats
   - Card image and information
   - **Add to Deck** section (if deck building is enabled)
   - **Manage Inventory** section

### 📦 **Adding Cards to Inventory**
In the Card Detail Modal, you'll find the **"Manage Inventory"** section with:

**Quantity Selection**:
- Choose how many copies you own (1-20)

**Condition Tracking**:
- **Mint** - Perfect condition
- **Near Mint** - Excellent condition  
- **Lightly Played** - Minor wear
- **Moderately Played** - Noticeable wear
- **Heavily Played** - Significant wear
- **Damaged** - Poor condition

**Personal Notes**:
- Add notes about where you got the card, price paid, or other details

**Current Ownership**:
- Shows if you already own the card and how many copies

### 🃏 **Adding Cards to Deck**
The same modal also includes **"Add to Deck"** functionality:
- Select quantity (1-4 for most cards, up to 20 for basic energy)
- One-click adding to your current deck

### 📊 **Inventory Features**
- **Real-time Updates**: Ownership status updates immediately
- **Multiple Conditions**: Track the same card in different conditions
- **Notes System**: Keep personal records about each card
- **Statistics**: View your collection stats on the inventory page
- **Export**: Export your collection to CSV

### 🚀 **Benefits of Unified System**
- **Single Location**: All card actions in one place
- **Better Context**: Full card details while making decisions
- **Streamlined Workflow**: No need to switch between multiple interfaces
- **Mobile Friendly**: Optimized for all screen sizes

### 📱 **Navigation**
- **Main Page**: Browse and search cards
- **Inventory Page**: View your complete collection (click "Inventory" in header)
- **Deck Builder**: Build and manage decks (click "Deck Builder" in header)

## Quick Start
1. Go to the main page
2. Click on any card you're interested in
3. In the modal, scroll down to "Manage Inventory"
4. Set quantity, condition, and notes
5. Click "Add to Inventory"
6. The card is now in your collection!

The unified modal system makes managing your PTCG collection easier and more efficient than ever before!
